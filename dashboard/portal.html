What is a community note?

A public note on your government’s to-do list.

A community note is a short, public record of a problem, written by the people who live with it. Each one carries a photo, a place and a date, and it stays open until the people responsible fix it. No more “we didn’t know.”

👀

Public by default

Anyone can see every issue, where it is, and what has been done about it.

🛡️

Private where it matters

We never show who reported. Phone numbers and photo locations are stripped.

📊

Accountable in numbers

Every LGA gets a public Trust Score from how fast it acts and fixes.

How it works

Three steps. One voice note.

01

See.

A crater on your road. A tap that’s been dry for weeks. A cable hanging low. If it’s broken, it’s worth a note.

02

Report.

Send a WhatsApp voice note in English or Yorùbá, plus a photo and your location or digital postcode. Our AI does the paperwork.

03

Resolve.

You get a reference number. Officers update it in public, and when they say it’s fixed, you confirm it.

  1. Citizens reportVoice note, photo, place
  2. AI prioritisesCategory, urgency, the right agency
  3. Officers actUpdates on the public record
  4. Citizens verifyOnly you can confirm it’s fixed

Complaints are never closed automatically. Officers can mark an issue fixed, but it only counts when the person who reported it agrees. If it isn’t fixed, one tap reopens it.
