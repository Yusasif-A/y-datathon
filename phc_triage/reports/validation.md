# PHC Triage — validation results (SYNTHETIC DATA)

> All numbers are from synthetic data generated by the PHC Triage simulator (real GRID3 locations, simulated reporting and outcomes). They show the method works under the simulator's assumptions; they are not evidence about real facilities.

**Target:** serious trouble in the next 1-2 months (team definition; see simulator/labels.py). **Test:** months 40–46 of the baseline world, 10,759 facility-months. Base rate 48%.

## Model vs baselines (baseline world, all LGAs)

| Method | Precision, top 5 per LGA | Recall, top 5 per LGA | Precision, top 20 per state | Early warning of new trouble |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Model** | 70% | 17% | 96% | 14% |
| Baseline (a): silent 2+ months | 55% | 13% | 70% | 14% |
| Baseline (b): low reporting rate | 58% | 14% | 78% | 9% |
| Random | 44% | 11% | 50% | 18% |

**Held-out LGAs only** (never seen in training): model 71% vs silent-2-months 51% vs low-rate 59% (precision, top 5 per LGA).

**Calibration:** Brier 0.2424, AUC 0.628.
